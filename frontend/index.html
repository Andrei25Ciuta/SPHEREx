How to spot a moving object
  1. Press Play — images from different dates flash one after another.
  2. Most stars stay put. Look for a dot that jumps or drifts.
  3. A dot that only gets brighter/fainter in place is probably a variable star.
  4. Slow down the speed or step frame by frame with ← →.
  5. Too faint? Try Difference (still stars vanish) or Trail (a mover leaves a line of dots).
  6. Found something? Click it to see its spectrum and light curve, and check the sky map: is it a known object?

3. Measure a source

Click a dot in the image. We measure its brightness in every frame: spectrum = brightness vs. wavelength (one colour per survey pass), light curve = brightness vs. date (one colour per detector).

Flux = background-subtracted sum in a 2-pixel aperture, in image units (not calibrated). A moving object is only inside the circle on the dates when it was there – that is expected, not an error.
